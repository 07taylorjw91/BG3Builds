Gale Origin Setup & Early Respec

  1. Nautiloid Prologue (15 mins): Gale starts locked as an Evocation Wizard. Prepare Friends and Charm Person to pass early dialogue checks on the ship.
  2. Unlock Withers at Level 2: On the beach, recruit Shadowheart and head straight into the Dank Crypt. Clear the undead room and open Withers' sarcophagus so he moves to camp.
  3. Respec & Racial Passive: Pay 100g to respec Gale into Mesmerist (you can pickpocket the gold right back from Withers). Choose Hearty as your Alternate Human passive for an always-on max HP buffer (check the Magic Mirror if locked at creation).

How Unarmored Gale Survives Melee in DIQ

  • Superior Defense + Shield (Level 4): Adds DEX + CHA + 1 (+1 every 5 levels) to unarmored AC while still allowing Gale's innate Human Shield proficiency. Hits 20 AC at Lv 4 and 26–32 AC late-game.
  • Parry & Riposte Stance (Level 6): Cuts incoming attack rolls by 1d10 + DEX + CHA (~14–16 penalty) and counterattacks misses.
  • 5+ Panache Banking (Level 9): Keeping 5+ Panache grants Evasion, Uncanny Dodge, and +1 Reaction point so you can proc Painful Stare without losing defensive reactions.
  • Critical Restoration (Level 8): Heals 1d6 (+1d6 per 5 levels) on every crit or kill with a light/1H piercing weapon.
  • Shield Gear Rule: Because you equip a Shield, skip Bracers of Defence and Bonespike Boots (both deactivate with a shield).
